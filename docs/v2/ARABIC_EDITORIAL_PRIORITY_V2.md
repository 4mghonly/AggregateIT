# AggregateIT V2 — Arabic editorial priority clarification (operator approved, 2026-10-29)

This document defines the **V2 target behavior**, not a claim that the current pipeline already implements it. Preserve the original V1 production rules and main branch unchanged until approved cutover.

1. **UAE is the sole geographic emphasis.** UAE-relevant developments may receive expanded explanation, provided evidence and relevance justify it.
2. **All other geographic areas are treated consistently.** Jordan, Palestine, Israel, and every other covered area receive no privileged or reduced allocation by name.
3. **Relevance first.** Rank eligible geopolitical/security events by verified consequence, timeliness, corroboration, severity, change from previous edition, and user-relevant impact, rather than by geographic quota or country-name weighting. Avoid treating publisher location as event location.
4. **Breadth without filler.** Capture the full range of most relevant events across the configured geographic scope. Do not manufacture an event to fill a regional slot; do not suppress a major development solely to preserve equal geographic representation.
5. **Evidence and language.** Preserve Arabic headline-to-summary integrity, original-language evidence, clear attribution, separate facts from analysis, and no unsupported prediction. In degraded mode, use publisher-attributed original Arabic content only.
6. **Presentation.** Three 3840x2160 Arabic slides; legible Noto Sans Arabic with correct RTL shaping; adapt density to evidence and add a supplementary annex if necessary. No LLM-generated visual assets.
7. **Release gate.** Compare selected stories with a region-blind relevance baseline (except explicit UAE emphasis), confirm broad coverage from independent eligible sources, and reject clipped/illegible pages. Current two-publisher fallback is insufficient to demonstrate complete geographic coverage.

The V1 `arabic_newsletter/PRODUCT_RULES.md` contains legacy wording about Jordan and Palestine/Israel; V2 must not treat that wording as a special editorial priority. This V2 clarification is authoritative for the replacement, while V1 production remains untouched.
