# Arabic Briefing Final Product Rules

These rules are production requirements, not style suggestions.

## 1. Headline integrity
- Every headline must describe exactly one underlying event.
- The headline must be a strict compression of the first sentence of the factual summary.
- The headline may not introduce an actor, location, action, target, quantity, attribution, certainty level, timing, or consequence that is absent from that sentence and unsupported by the cited evidence.
- Generic thematic, sensational, predictive, or composite headlines are prohibited.
- If two developments are materially different, publish two events or omit one; never merge them under one headline.
- Headline maximum: 100 characters.

## 2. Summary integrity
- Sentence one must explain the same event as the headline and include attribution where appropriate.
- Later sentences may add supported context but may not change the event.
- Numbers and quantities must preserve the source value and scale.
- Disputed or unverified claims must remain attributed.
- No finance, market, lifestyle, routine crime, or unrelated-region material.

## 3. Evidence and provenance
- Every event requires one or more article IDs and literal evidence excerpts from those exact articles.
- Every cited source must contribute evidence.
- The rendered source line appears immediately under the headline.
- Publisher location never determines the event region; the subject/location of the event does.

## 4. Analysis separation
- Factual summary and analytical assessment are separate fields.
- Assessment may make cautious inference only from validated events and must signal inference explicitly.
- Analysis must never introduce new actors, quantities, dates, events, intentions, or causal claims.
- "What to watch" must identify observable indicators, not predictions.

## 5. Layout
- Three slides remain the production format.
- Each visible story appears once across news slots.
- Priority stories use shorter titles and fuller summaries; secondary stories use tighter summaries.
- Source attribution sits directly below the headline.
- UAE coverage remains expanded but follows the same headline/evidence rules.
- Jordan and Palestine/Israel remain covered without oversized standalone treatment.
- Text must not overlap, clip materially, or use unsupported glyphs.
- Approved production font: Noto Sans Arabic.
- Production render: 3840x2160 PNG.

## 6. Degraded-mode rules
- LLM/editor failure may not create invented substitute analysis.
- The fallback may use only successfully collected Arabic-language source material.
- In fallback mode, the publisher's own Arabic headline becomes the visible title.
- Summary sentence one must explicitly attribute and restate that same headline claim.
- The product must disclose that advanced automated analysis was unavailable for that cycle.

## 7. Publication gate
An event is rejected when any of the following occurs:
- headline/summary mismatch;
- unsupported citation or evidence;
- unsupported number or quantity;
- wrong geography;
- non-Arabic final prose;
- financial/out-of-scope content;
- duplicate development;
- editorial-review rejection.

Prefer omission to a misleading or weakly supported story.
